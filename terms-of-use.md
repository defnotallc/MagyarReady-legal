## 2. Description of Service

MagyarReady is an iOS application designed to help users learn Hungarian language and prepare for the Hungarian simplified naturalization (egyszerűsített honosítás) citizenship interview. The App provides:

- Hungarian vocabulary flash cards
- Interview preparation question banks and personalized answers
- Legal document guidance for the citizenship application process
- English-Hungarian translation tools
- Practice conversation scenarios
- A personal profile system that generates Hungarian phrases from your information
