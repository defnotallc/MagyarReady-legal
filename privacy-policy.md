## Overview

MagyarReady ("we," "our," or "the app") is a Hungarian language and citizenship interview preparation app for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our app.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This app is designed with a **local-first** approach — your personal profile data stays on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you use the Profile section of MagyarReady, you may enter personal information including:

- Your name and Hungarian name variant
- Date of birth and place of birth
- Current address
- Marital status and family information (spouse, children, parents, grandparents, siblings)
- Hungarian ancestry information (ancestor name, birthplace, emigration history)
- Career and education information (occupation, employer, school, field of study)
- Hungarian language level and learning methods
- Reason for citizenship application

**This information is stored exclusively on your device** using Apple's UserDefaults system. It is never uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties, except as needed to generate content within the app (such as personalized interview answers and Hungarian practice phrases).

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Google AdMob (Advertising)**

MagyarReady uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA), device information, and usage data to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance.

AdMob's data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy:  
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Users in applicable regions will see a consent dialog (powered by Google's User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown. You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device's privacy settings under **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.

**Apple Analytics and Crash Reporting**

If you have enabled "Share Analytics" in your iOS settings, Apple may collect anonymized usage and crash data from your device. This data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy:  
https://www.apple.com/privacy/

### 1.3 In-App Purchases

MagyarReady offers optional in-app purchases (monthly subscription and lifetime access) processed by **Apple App Store**. Apple handles all payment processing. We do not collect or store your payment information. Apple's data practices are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy:  
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

### 1.4 Amazon Affiliate Links

MagyarReady contains affiliate links to Amazon.com products. When you tap a link, you are directed to Amazon's website. Amazon may collect information as described in Amazon's Privacy Notice:  
https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GX7NJQ4ZB8MHFRNJ

We receive a small commission from qualifying Amazon purchases made through these links, at no additional cost to you (affiliate tag: defnota-20).

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Information you enter in your Profile is used **solely within the app** to:

- Generate personalized Hungarian practice phrases based on your name, family, and background
- Personalize interview question answers for your specific citizenship application situation
- Calculate profile completion percentage shown in the Home and Profile tabs

**We do not use your personal profile data for advertising targeting, analytics, or any purpose outside the app.**

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security

Your profile data is stored locally on your iOS device using Apple's UserDefaults framework. It is:

- **Not transmitted to any server**
- **Not backed up to our cloud** (standard iCloud device backup may include this data — subject to your iCloud backup settings)
- **Protected by iOS device encryption** when your device is locked
- **Deletable** at any time using "Reset All Profile Data" in the Profile tab, or by deleting the app

We recommend using a device passcode or Face ID/Touch ID to protect your device.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We share limited data with:

| Party | What | Why |
|---|---|---|

## 5. Children's Privacy

MagyarReady is designed for adults 17 years of age and older who are preparing for the Hungarian citizenship interview. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through our app, please contact us at defnota.official@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

**Access and Deletion:**  
All personal data you enter is accessible and deletable at any time within the app. Navigate to Profile → "Reset All Profile Data" to permanently delete all stored profile information.

**Advertising Opt-Out:**  
You may limit ad tracking by going to **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking** on your iOS device. For California residents, you may opt out of the sale of personal information (though we do not sell personal data directly).

**GDPR (European Economic Area / UK):**  
If you are located in the EEA or UK, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing of, and port your personal data. Since all profile data is stored locally on your device, you control it directly. Contact us at defnota.official@gmail.com for any questions.

**CCPA (California):**  
California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete it, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights. We do not sell personal information.
