## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**What is MagyarReady?**  
MagyarReady is an iOS app that helps people of Hungarian descent learn the Hungarian language and prepare for the simplified naturalization (egyszerűsített honosítás) citizenship interview at the Hungarian consulate.

**Do I need an internet connection?**  
No! All flash cards, interview questions, and conversation scenarios work fully offline. The Translation tab requires an internet connection only for the initial Hungarian language pack download — after that it works offline too.

---

### Profile & Personalization

**How do I personalize my interview answers?**  
Fill in your profile on the Profile tab with your name, family details, ancestry, and background. The Interview Prep tab will automatically generate personalized Hungarian answers tailored to your specific situation.

**How do I reset my profile data?**  
Go to Profile → scroll to the bottom → tap **Reset All Profile Data**. This permanently deletes all your stored profile information from the device.

**Is my personal data safe?**  
Yes. All profile data is stored exclusively on your device using iOS local storage. It is never uploaded to any server or shared with any third party. See our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for full details.

### Purchases & Subscriptions

**What does the premium subscription include?**  
Premium removes all advertisements from the app. You can subscribe monthly ($9.99/month) or purchase lifetime access ($99.99 one-time).

**How do I restore a previous purchase?**  
Tap **Go Premium** on any screen showing ads, then tap **Restore Purchase** at the bottom of the paywall sheet.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**  
Open **iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → MagyarReady → Cancel Subscription**. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

**I was charged but ads are still showing. What do I do?**  
Force-quit the app and relaunch it. If ads still appear, tap **Go Premium → Restore Purchase**. If the issue persists, contact us at defnota.official@gmail.com with your Apple Order ID.

**Can I get a refund?**  
Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) to request a refund.

### Flash Cards

**How does the learned/struggling tracking work?**  
Tap the **✓ Learned** button when you know a card confidently. Tap **⚡ Struggling** to flag cards you want to review more. Your progress is saved automatically.

**Can I reset my flash card progress?**  
Not yet — this is a planned feature. As a workaround you can delete and reinstall the app, but this will also reset your profile.

---

### Translation

**The Translation tab says I need to download a language pack. How do I do that?**  
The app uses Apple's on-device Translation framework. When you first use the Translate tab, iOS will offer to download the Hungarian language pack (approximately 200MB). You need Wi-Fi for this download. After downloading, translation works fully offline.

---

### Privacy & Advertising

**Why does the app ask for permission to track me?**  
MagyarReady uses Google AdMob for advertising in the free version. iOS requires apps to ask your permission before using your device's advertising identifier. You can decline and the app will still work — you'll just see non-personalized ads.

**How do I opt out of personalized ads?**  
Go to **iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking** and turn off tracking for MagyarReady. You can also adjust this in **iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising**.
